Four new rooms

Cozy, a reworked Arcade, a maximalist one and an elegant one. Each is a full chat mockup in the Lantern shell; flip any of them between day and night with the switch on its plaque.

I.

Hearthside cozy, warm

A mountain inn during a snowstorm. A knitted blanket for a sidebar, chats as gingham patches, portraits in embroidery hoops, stitched cards, a window with the weather in it, steam when someone's typing. Day: Sunday Morning, oat and terracotta in window light. Night: Fireside, with embers glowing from the corner and snow falling outside.

II.

Arcade Afterhours reworked

From the 70s woodgrain cabinet to a 90s game-centre fighting game. The chat list is a character-select roster, the header is a versus bar with health and a round timer, speech comes in slanted panels with hard shadows, and send is a real arcade button. Red, gold and royal blue; still no cyan or magenta. Day: Attract Mode, a sunburst on yellow. Night: High Score, a city skyline under scanlines.

IV.

Atelier elegant, mature

A literary journal rather than a chat app. No bubbles: the story is set as a manuscript, with a drop cap, small-caps names, a fleuron rule, your lines indented on a thin claret rule, and story notes as marginalia. Chats are a table of contents with dotted leaders. Kept clear of Noir Salon: no gilt, no Deco, no black lacquer. Day: Linen. Night: Velvet, aubergine and champagne.
