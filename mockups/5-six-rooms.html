Six Rooms

Six new ways for Mea to look, each borrowed from a different wing of the museum. Every room has a day and a night, the same evening by the sea inside (one of them in Westeros), and a token set that drops straight into tokens.css.

Switch any room between day and night, or choose Compare and drag the seam across the app.
